This file records the *moment of recognition* in Omar's visual taste. It is deliberately **not** a client-facing brand book, design-system rule, automatic UI preset, or an instruction to imitate a single photographer.

## The actual moment

1. Omar shared a personal Instagram-story screenshot around **04:47**: a low-angle self-portrait with curly hair, a dark patterned shirt, strongly blown-out backlight and a shadowed face. The conversation highlighted the unpolished, human, accidentally beautiful feeling.
2. Asked whether he loved both the visual *feeling* and the strange/imperfect/absurd character of an image, he explicitly answered: **«عاشق الاتنين»** — *both*.
3. Four visual territories were discussed: **Anti-Fashion** (Juergen Teller), **Intimate Grunge** (Corinne Day), **Everyday Absurdism** (Martin Parr), and **Beautifully Strange** (Ren Hang). These are research leads, not four independently confirmed favorites.
4. Around **04:51**, Omar sent a screenshot focused on two photographs shown in the **Corinne Day / Intimate Grunge** section and said: **«انا بعشق دي بقا»**. This is the **strongest confirmed artist/image affinity** in this conversation.
5. He then asked to document the photos, the conversation and his taste across GitHub and projects.

## What is confirmed vs what is interpretation

**Confirmed by Omar:** he loves both photographic feeling and visual oddness; candid, imperfect photography resonates; the specific two Corinne Day reference photographs resonated strongly; he wants this preserved as long-term, revisable creative context.

**Our visual reading (interpretive, not a universal preference statement):**
- Intimate / lived-in / raw portraiture: the everyday room, body, expression, furniture, washed-out wall.
- Unpolished photographic texture: direct flash or existing light, strong highlights, shadow, grain, soft mobile-camera rendering.
- Anti-fashion sensibility: presence rather than a perfected commercial pose.
- Everyday absurdity and beautifully strange composition.
- Deliberate editorial judgment that does **not** erase the feeling of actual life.

## Project-use protocol

- **Photography, film, personal editorial work:** actively research closeness, candid life, imperfect light, non-performative expression, unforced strangeness. Try competing directions; the taste is a starting reference, not a finished brief.
- **Web, portfolio, cultural work, art direction:** let this influence *curation and investigation* where the actual project supports it. Validate project reality, people, source media, accessibility, audience, evidence and meaning.
- **Operational systems, engineering, B2B software:** do **not** impose grunge, noise, dark palettes or unconventional interaction without a user need. Functionality, clarity, safety and actual product truth come first.
- **Critical constraints:** no formulaic 'AI cinematic' styling, no automatic grain filters, no copying Corinne Day's compositions, no forced sadness, no default anti-polish dogma, and no assumption that this exhausts his taste.

**Operating method:** real material → competing research directions → visual test → critique → fit decision → refinement.
