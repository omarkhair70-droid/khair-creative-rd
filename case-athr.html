CASE 001 / HOSPITALITY, SPACE, CULTURECREATIVE EXPLORATION · WEB EXECUTION
ATHR.

From space
to experience.

A self-initiated exploration into how a café can carry a story — through atmosphere, physical intervention, visual language and optional interaction.

THE QUESTION

What if a place could be remembered for more than how it looked?

01 / THE REALITY

The space came first.

Rather than beginning with an app or a visual trend, ATHR began with observing hospitality environments, studying their corners, surfaces, guest behavior and possibilities.

01

Observe a real space

Owner conversations, on-site attention and visual documentation were part of the original exploration, as described in the project history.

02

Identify an emotional opening

What could a guest notice, pause at, share, or remember? How much can change without a complete renovation?

03

Develop propositions

Walls, lighting, projection, interaction and designed corners became candidate interventions, rather than predetermined deliverables.
